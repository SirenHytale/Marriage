# Marriage v2.3.0 - Changelog (CurseForge)

Paste the block below into the CurseForge "Changelog" field when uploading the v2.3.0 file.

---

## <span style="color: #b96ad9;"><strong>v2.3.0 - Update 7 Compatibility &amp; the Wedding Dress</strong></span>

<span style="color: #eccafa;">Marriage is ready for Hytale's Update 7 - and to celebrate, there is finally something to get married in. 2.3 also closes a permission hole that let any player use the admin commands, so every server should update.</span>

<span style="color: #843fa1;">===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===</span>

### <span style="color: #b96ad9;"><strong>New: the Wedding Dress</strong></span>

*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">👰 A new chest armor piece: a white satin gown with long lace sleeves, a pearl-edged sweetheart neckline, a blush sash tied in a bow at the back, and a floor-length skirt that sweeps behind you and sways as you walk.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">Crafted at the <strong>Armor Bench</strong> (Chest) from <strong>6 Silk Bolts</strong>, <strong>4 Cotton Bolts</strong> and <strong>1 Diamond</strong>. It protects like the vanilla Silk Tunic.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">While you wear it, your shirt and over-pants cosmetics are hidden, so nothing pokes through the lace or the skirt.</span>

<span style="color: #843fa1;">===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===</span>

### <span style="color: #b96ad9;"><strong>Fixes</strong></span>

*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">🔒 <strong>Security:</strong> <strong>/marriage force marry | divorce | info</strong> and <strong>/marriage requirering</strong> could be run by every player, not just admins. They now need their admin permissions again.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">The <strong>Cmd-Permission</strong> config option now really gates the player commands - before, turning it on still let everyone use them.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">Accepting a proposal from a player in a different world no longer fails partway through - the ring tier, the rings and the announcement all happen as they should.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">The respawn-at-your-partner perk no longer collides with the normal respawn.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">The couple chat channel keeps its pink style when Cultivation or Siren's Commands is installed alongside Marriage.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">The update check can no longer stall a server: it gives up after a few seconds if the website doesn't answer, and it no longer holds up server startup.</span>

<span style="color: #843fa1;">===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===</span>

### <span style="color: #b96ad9;"><strong>Compatibility</strong></span>

*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">🔧 Updated for <strong>Hytale Server 0.7.0-pre.5.1</strong> (Update 7). This release no longer runs on Update 6 servers - stay on <strong>v2.2.0</strong> if your server hasn't updated yet. Back up your worlds before upgrading: a world saved on the pre-release isn't guaranteed to open on Update 6 again.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">In Update 7, a damage-over-time effect put on you by an attack or ability now counts as damage from whoever applied it. So one your spouse lands on you counts as theirs: couple PvP protection blocks it and Bond of Pain doesn't share it - and a kill made with such an effect, with your partner nearby, now earns co-op bond XP.</span>

<span style="color: #843fa1;">===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===</span>
