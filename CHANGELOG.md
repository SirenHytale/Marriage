# Marriage v2.1.0 - Changelog (CurseForge)

Paste the block below into the CurseForge "Changelog" field when uploading the v2.1.0 file.

---

## <span style="color: #b96ad9;"><strong>v2.1.0 - Somewhere To Go</strong></span>

<span style="color: #eccafa;">2.0 made being married do something. 2.1 makes it do <em>more</em> the longer it lasts, and asks you what kind of marriage you want it to be.</span>

<span style="color: #eccafa;"><strong>Existing marriages carry over untouched.</strong> Their bond starts at level 0 and begins counting the first time either partner logs in on 2.1.0 - no back-pay, no reset, nothing to re-run. Your config is upgraded in place to version 5, and every new value arrives with its description beside it.</span>

<span style="color: #843fa1;">===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===</span>

### <span style="color: #b96ad9;"><strong>The Bond</strong></span>

<span style="color: #eccafa;">Every marriage now has a <strong>bond</strong> that deepens with the hours you are married, the minutes you spend within reach of each other, the gifts you hand over and the things you kill side by side.</span>

*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">A new <strong>Our Bond</strong> page: your level, how far through it you are, how long you have spent side by side, when your next anniversary falls, and the full ladder of perks. <strong>/marry bond</strong> says the same in chat.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">Levels are worth something on their own - partner regeneration <strong>reaches further</strong> and the partner teleport <strong>cools down faster</strong> with each one.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">A <strong>standing</strong> that grows with it: Newlyweds, Devoted, Steadfast, Inseparable, Eternal.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">A <strong>honeymoon</strong> - double bond XP for your first day married.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;"><strong>Anniversaries</strong> at 1, 7, 30 and 365 days, announced to the server, and paid in <strong>gifts you can hold</strong>: a Love Letter at a week, a Gold Band at a month, a Diamond Band at a year. A partner who was away still finds theirs waiting.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">Optional <strong>bond decay</strong> for servers that want the bond to mean "we actually see each other" - off by default.</span>

<span style="color: #843fa1;">===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===</span>

### <span style="color: #b96ad9;"><strong>Seven Perks Worth Staying Married For</strong></span>

<span style="color: #eccafa;">Each unlocks at its own bond level and has its own switch. Set a level to 0 to hand that perk out on the wedding day, or turn any of them off entirely.</span>

*   <span style="color: #eccafa;"><strong>Death Alert</strong> - when your partner falls you are told where, and the spot is pinned on your map. It stays there while they are offline, which is exactly when you need it.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;"><strong>Partner on the Map</strong> - their live position, in rose, on your world map.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;"><strong>Shared Home</strong> - one home the two of you share. Either can move it, the other is told, and it travels across worlds.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;"><strong>Bond of Pain</strong> - a share of the damage aimed at one of you lands on the other instead. It <em>moves</em> harm rather than creating it.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;"><strong>Last Stand</strong> - when your partner falls beside you, their strength carries you a moment longer.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;"><strong>Hope Chest</strong> - one container you both reach from anywhere. Both of you can have it open at once, from different worlds.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;"><strong>Return to Your Beloved</strong> - rise beside your living partner instead of at your bed.</span>

<span style="color: #843fa1;">===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===</span>

### <span style="color: #b96ad9;"><strong>Vows - Every Marriage Its Own</strong></span>

<span style="color: #eccafa;">Two couples at the same bond level used to have the same marriage. Now you swear a <strong>vow</strong>, and it decides what your bond is best at:</span>

*   <span style="color: #eccafa;"><strong>Vow of the Hearth</strong> - a home and a chest, sooner and larger.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;"><strong>Vow of the Blade</strong> - what one of you takes, the other shares. Harder, and sooner.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;"><strong>Vow of Wandering</strong> - never far apart for long: quicker to reach each other, and to rise beside them.</span>

<span style="color: #eccafa;">A vow is <strong>chosen once and held</strong> - it is a promise, not a loadout. <strong>Renewing your vows</strong> is what lets you choose again: after long enough together, both of you ask, and you get a surge of bond, a reset decay clock, a fresh vow, and your band upgraded to the finest one you are holding.</span>

<span style="color: #843fa1;">===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===</span>

### <span style="color: #b96ad9;"><strong>Rings, Letters, and Little Things</strong></span>

*   <span style="color: #eccafa;"><strong>Ring tiers.</strong> The old ring is now the <strong>Copper Band</strong>; craft it up into <strong>Gold</strong> and <strong>Diamond</strong>. A finer band grows the bond faster AND knocks levels off every perk requirement. The <em>lower</em> of the two bands counts - a Diamond band is worth nothing unless your partner brought one too.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;"><strong>Engraved rings.</strong> If the wedding does not consume the bands, they come away carrying both your names.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;"><strong>Love Letters.</strong> Craft one, write in it, seal it. The words live on the item, so it survives being dropped, stored, gifted and the server restarting - and the tooltip says who it is from. Gift it; they read it whenever they like.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">A <strong>couple channel</strong> that stays on - <strong>/marry channel</strong>, and everything you type goes to your partner until you toggle it back. No command per line.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;"><strong>Emotes</strong> - <strong>/marry hug</strong>, <strong>/marry kiss</strong>, <strong>/marry dance</strong>. Hearts, a sound, and a line for anyone close enough to have seen it.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">A <strong>partner plate</strong> showing your partner's health and distance while they are near.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">A <strong>Records</strong> page - longest marriage still standing, longest that ever was, most generous, most married, and most divorced.</span>

<span style="color: #843fa1;">===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===</span>

### <span style="color: #b96ad9;"><strong>Divorce Finally Costs Something</strong></span>

*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">If you <strong>both</strong> want out it is instant, as it should be.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">One-sided, it is <strong>filed</strong>: your partner is told, and can <strong>agree</strong> to end it at once or <strong>contest</strong> it. Contesting does not block the divorce - nobody should be able to trap somebody in a marriage - it makes it serve the full waiting period instead of the shortened one. The filer can withdraw.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">Afterwards there is a <strong>remarry cooldown</strong>, and the marriage is <strong>remembered</strong>: who, how long, and when.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">A divorce <strong>splits the Hope Chest</strong> stack by stack between you. Whoever is offline gets their half on their next login. Nothing is ever destroyed.</span>

<span style="color: #843fa1;">===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===</span>

### <span style="color: #b96ad9;"><strong>The Menu Does Everything Now</strong></span>

<span style="color: #eccafa;">Every /marry command has a button. You never have to type one again.</span>

*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">A <strong>Propose</strong> page listing everyone online you could ask, one click each. Somebody who has already asked <em>you</em> shows as <strong>Accept</strong>, because clicking it marries you on the spot. People who have proposals turned off are greyed with the reason, whoever you have already asked appears with a live countdown, and if a ring is required the page says so before you click.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">Teleport, gift, love note, letter, home, set home, forget home, Hope Chest, emotes, renew, divorce - all on your marriage page.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">Admins get <strong>/marriage force marry</strong>, <strong>/marriage force divorce</strong> and <strong>/marriage force info</strong>, plus a Divorce control on every row of the Married Couples page. Divorce and info work on <strong>offline</strong> players.</span>

<span style="color: #843fa1;">===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===</span>

### <span style="color: #b96ad9;"><strong>For Server Owners & Mod Authors</strong></span>

*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">The config grew by <strong>over 50 values</strong>, each with its description beside it and every one editable in game - the Admin page gained fourteen new sections for them.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">Want the perks without the progression? Turn <strong>Enable-Bond</strong> off and every enabled perk is simply available from the wedding day.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">The <strong>MarriageAPI</strong> gained getBondLevel, getBondProgress, addBondXp and hasPerk, and MarriageInfo now carries the bond level, XP, time together and anniversaries reached. <strong>MarriageEvents</strong> gained bond level changes, anniversaries, and shared homes being set.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">English and Chinese, both complete.</span>

<span style="color: #843fa1;">===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===--===</span>

### <span style="color: #b96ad9;"><strong>Notes</strong></span>

*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">Built for Hytale server <strong>0.5.x</strong>.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">"Time married" only accrues while at least one partner is online - so a couple cannot reach the deepest bond by logging off for a month.</span>
*   <span style="color: #eccafa;">Running <strong>Cultivation</strong>? Its Partnered Cultivation (双修) has read marriages through this API since its 0.6.0 - and now the link runs both ways. A <strong>Dual Cultivation</strong> page appears in the Marriage menu on its own, showing your Qi multiplier, how close you must sit, the realm you need, and whether the two of you are dual cultivating right now. On a server without Cultivation the page never appears.</span>
